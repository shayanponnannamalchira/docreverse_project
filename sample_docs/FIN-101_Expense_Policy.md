# Expense Policy

Owner: Finance Manager
Version: 3.2
Last Reviewed: 2024-02-15
Department: Finance

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines which business expenses employees may claim and how they are approved. It supports the Expense Reimbursement SOP (FIN-102).

## 2. Approval Limits
Employees must submit an Expense Report for every claim. The Line Manager approves claims up to $2,500. The Finance Manager approves claims over $2,500. Any claim over $10,000 requires approval from the Chief Financial Officer.

Managers must approve or reject a submitted claim within 3 business days. Claims that are not approved within 3 business days are escalated to the Finance Team.

## 3. Prohibited Expenses
Employees must not claim personal travel, alcohol, or fines. Receipts are mandatory for any expense over $25.

## 4. Reimbursement
The Payroll Team pays approved claims in the next payroll run. Payment is made within 10 days of final approval. Claims are recorded in Concur and archived in SharePoint.

## 5. Glossary
- SLA: service level agreement, the promised response time for a request.
- Concur: the corporate expense management platform.
