Vendor Onboarding Process

Owner: Procurement Manager

Process

A new Vendor must be approved before any Purchase Order is issued.

  1. The Requester submits a Vendor Application Form to the Procurement Team.
  2. The Procurement Team performs a due diligence check and records the result in SAP.
  3. The Legal Team reviews the Vendor Agreement.
  4. If the annual spend is over $50,000, the Chief Financial Officer must approve the Vendor.
  5. The Procurement Manager activates the Vendor in SAP.

Vendors are reviewed every 12 months. Approved Vendors must be re-assessed within 5 business days of a security incident.
