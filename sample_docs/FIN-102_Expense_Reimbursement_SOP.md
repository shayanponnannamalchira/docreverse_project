# Expense Reimbursement SOP

Process Owner: Accounts Payable Supervisor
Version: 1.4
Last Reviewed: 2023-01-10

## Purpose
Describes how an expense claim moves from submission to payment. See the Expense Policy for limits.

## Procedure
When an employee returns from a business trip, the following steps apply.

1. Employee creates the Expense Report in Concur and attaches all receipts.
2. Employee submits the Expense Report to the Line Manager.
3. Line Manager reviews the claim and approves it within 5 business days.
4. If the claim is over $2,500, the Finance Manager must also approve it in Concur.
5. Accounts Payable verifies the receipts and codes the expense in SAP.
6. Payroll Team schedules payment in the next payroll run.
7. Employee receives a confirmation email from Concur.

## Escalation
If a claim is rejected, the Line Manager notifies the Employee and records the reason in Concur. For disputes, contact the Finance Team or see HR-204 for the grievance process.
